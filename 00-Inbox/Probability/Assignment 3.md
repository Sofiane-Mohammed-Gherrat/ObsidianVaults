**QUESTION 1 (15 marks)**
Suppose \(X\) and \(Y\) are random variables with  
\(P(X = 1) = P(X = -1) = \frac{1}{2};\) \(P(Y = 1) = P(Y = -1) = \frac{1}{2}\)  
Let \(c = P(X = 1 \text{ and } Y = 1)\)  

(a) Determine the joint distribution of \(X\) and \(Y\), \(\text{Cov}(X,Y)\), and \(\text{Corr}(X,Y)\) in terms of \(c\) [10 marks]  

(b) Determine the value of \(c\) such that \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent. [2 marks]  

(c) Determine the value(s) of \(c\) such that \(X\) and \(Y\) have positive and negative perfect correlation. [3 marks]

A science student is observing a mouse in the following simple maze:

```plaintext
        |              |               |
        +---------+    |               |
		          |    |      6        |
				  |                    |
			      +--------------------+
				   
```

During each period under observation, the mouse moves to any adjacent, accessible compartment with equal probability. Assume the mouse is consistently moving all the time, i.e., not staying idle at any compartment. Successive moves have the Markov property. Let \(X_t\) be the compartment number that the mouse is in at time \(t\).

(a) Draw a transition diagram, including the probabilities of the possible transitions. [5 marks]  

(b) Evaluate the probability that a mouse which starts in compartment 4 at time 0 is also in compartment 4:  
 i. at time 1;  
 ii. at time 2;  
 iii. at time 3. [5 marks]

**QUESTION 4 (10 marks)**

A customer of a certain region classified as Urban (U), Suburban (S) or Rural (R). A marketing company has found that over successive 5 year periods, customers choose from one classification to another according to the probabilities given by the following matrix:

|     | U   | S   | R   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U   | 0.7 | 0.1 | 0.2 |
| S   | 0.1 | 0.8 | 0.1 |
| R   | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.8 |


(a) Draw the state transition diagram for the matrix. [3 marks]  

(b) Find the probability that a suburban customer will be a rural customer in 15 years. [4 marks]  

(c) Suppose the population of the region is \(50\%\) urban, \(25\%\) suburban and \(25\%\) rural. Determine the expected population distribution in 15 years. [3 marks]

1 (11 marks) Let the cumulative distribution function of a random variable \(Y\) be

\[
F(y) = 
\begin{cases} 
0 & y < 0 \\ 
\frac{y}{4} & 0 \leq y < 1 \\ 
\frac{1}{2} + \frac{y-1}{4} & 1 \leq y < 3 \\ 
1 & y \geq 3 
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Find probability density function of \(Y\). [2]

(b) Find \(P(1 \leq Y \leq 3)\) [1]

(c) Find \(P(Y \geq 1.5)\) [1]

(d) Find \(P(Y \geq 1 \mid Y \leq 3)\) [2]

(e) Find the mean and variance of \(Y\) [5]

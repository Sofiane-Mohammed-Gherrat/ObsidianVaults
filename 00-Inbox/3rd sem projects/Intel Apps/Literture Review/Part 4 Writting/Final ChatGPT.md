## 4.1 Introduction

Caregivers of children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) often face considerable challenges when trying to access reliable information and timely support (Chu et al., 2020; Dobrogowska-Schlebusch, 2016; Mohd Hussain et al., 2022). In the Malaysian context, these difficulties appear particularly pronounced, with many caregivers reporting uncertainty when interpreting diagnoses, identifying appropriate interventions, and managing the broader effects of ASD on family life (Chu et al., 2020).

The literature suggests that these challenges operate at several interconnected levels rather than arising from a single source. One set of issues relates to information and knowledge barriers, where caregivers may begin their search with limited understanding of ASD and unequal access to reliable information, often shaped by educational and socioeconomic circumstances (Chu et al., 2020; Dobrogowska-Schlebusch, 2016; Mohd Hussain et al., 2022). At the same time, digital platforms have become an increasingly important source of guidance and support, raising questions about how caregivers assess the credibility of what they encounter online (Dobrogowska-Schlebusch, 2016; Twombly et al., 2011).

The literature also points toward psychological and social dimensions of caregiving. Experiences of stigma, reduced social participation, and the emotional demands associated with raising a child with ASD may influence how caregivers engage with available support systems, even if the exact relationship between emotional state and information-seeking remains unclear (Papadopoulos et al., 2019; Chu et al., 2020). Beyond these individual experiences, structural factors such as unequal access to specialist services and the financial burden of long-term care continue to shape the caregiving landscape (Kalkbrenner et al., 2011; Ou et al., 2015).

Taken together, these studies paint a picture of caregiving that is both information-intensive and context-dependent. Understanding how these different challenges intersect provides a useful foundation for considering technology-based approaches that aim to improve access to ASD-related information and support.


## AutiGuide: An Intelligent Chatbot for Parents of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder           

### Project Proposal

### 1. Introduction

Chikhi Mohamed Yahia

Autism Spectrum disorder also known as ASD is an intricate neurodevelopmental condition that causes recurring challenges and obstacles in communication of many forms, high sensitivity and repetitive behavioral patterns. According to statistics published by the World Health Organisation in 2023, approximately 1 in 100 children globally is diagnosed with Autism Spectrum disorder, with rising rates in both developed and developing countries. In Malaysia particularly, awareness of ASD has been growing these past years, but parents and guardians of autistic children still find it hard to find quick, useful and practical help to support the child’s constant needs and growth.

Taking care of a child with ASD comes with a lot of stressful and peculiar challenges that exceed what most families were prepared for. Caregivers often go through difficulties when managing behavioral patterns, learning about available medical and therapeutic actions to amend these episodes, approaching the education system, and finding local support resources. Even though professional doctors and experienced therapists exist to provide this help, booked consultations are usually expensive, not always available, and hard to access for families living in rural or underdeveloped communities. This dichotomy of urgent need for support, information, advice and the availability of professionals pushed the need for a smart, accessible, and always present assistance tool.

In many other fields, the potential of chatbot and rule based expert systems has been proven to be effective at solving these complications. Jusoh et al. (2024) developed HelpBot, a web based chatbot using natural language processing (NLP) combined with a template based form to help adolescents who suffer from depression using personalized support. Their work and research showed that smart chatbots can be used as helpful companions for populations who are vulnerable through providing instant, empathetic, and useful output without really replacing the care of professionals. Our app AutiGuide uses a similar thought process and concrete approach, bringing this technology to more underserved communities like the parents and guardians of people with autism.

AutiGuide will be a retrieval based intelligent chatbot also including a rule based expert system designed specifically for caregivers of children with ASD. The system will give immediate, scientifically accurate and empathetic responses in English to inquiries about managing certain behaviors, therapeutic advice, diet, habits, help in school, and local specifically Malaysian resources. By taking advantage of natural language processing methods and a rich knowledge base, AutiGuide’s goal is to help parents feel more understood and empower them with valuable information and advice to make them more confident and handle their children at all times, without it being inaccessible, costly or taboo.

### 2. Rationale of the Application

Yessimov Jamil 

Nowadays, there are 3 critical existing gaps in the Malaysian support ecosystem of autistic kids showing the actual demand for AutiGuide.

First, not every caregiver can obtain the required knowledge. Trained therapists and professionals are not accessed by a large proportion of guardians, typically living in underdeveloped or lower-income communities. Pazey and Cole (2013) have revealed that parents of children with such disability often feel lonely and uninformed which forces them to obey recommendations from unreliable sources, such as online forums and social media groups. Potential misleading information fetch from those can result into harmful practices that would, indeed, impact the trajectory of child’s development.

Second, behavioural crises need to be acutely treated or resolved. The hardest and the most stressful part of taking care of autistic child/person is trying to handle sudden episodes of meltdowns, sensory overloads, etc. These cases require immediate evidence-based action from guardians that cannot be obtained via normal appointment with a specialist; hence, it is hard to underestimate the value of 24/7 available chatbot. Via the development of  HelpBot Jusoh et al (2024) showcased that template-based NLPs are able to provide timely and professional support to users experiencing distress, effectively tackling the problem of the shortage of mental helth therapists. The same principle is used in AutiGuide whenever immediate guidance is needed,

Third, no dedicated intelligent system currently exists for autism caregivers in Malaysia. Existing general-purpose chatbots and mental health applications do not address the precise and nuanced needs of parents raising autistic children. The work of Jusoh et al. (2024) showed that chatbot efforts tend to focus on particular target problems and that more specialized efforts are necessary to serve different vulnerable populations. AutiGuide addresses this directly by focusing uniquely on the autism caregiving sectors in the English language, establishing that all responses are relevant, accurate, and practically grounded for English-speaking caregivers and parents seeking dependable digital support.

The social influence of AutiGuide is not constrained to individual families. By raising awareness around the disability and its treatment, the application will take part in enhancing development of children with the condition. Moreover, it will support Malaysia’s national commitment to inclusive education and disability welfare as outlined in the Persons with Disabilities Act 2008. 

### 3. Objectives

Aissat Fethi Malik

The goals of AutiGuide are as follows: 

1. Design and development of an intelligent chatbot for retrieving answers to commonly asked questions in English from the parents and caregivers of children suffering from Autism Spectrum Disorder.
    
2. Creation of a complete and validated database of information in English on five major aspects of caregiving for a child with ASD, including behavioral management, interventions, nutritional advice, educational and socialization strategies, and information on autism in Malaysia.
    
3. Use of natural language processing techniques to understand the intent of English queries made by the parents and retrieve the best available information from the database.
    
4. Testing the effectiveness and usability of AutiGuide in user testing sessions with targeted users to measure response accuracy and user satisfaction.
    
5. Ensuring that the information provided by AutiGuide is based on sound research and is responsibly provided as informational guidance, not a medical diagnosis or treatment.
    

### 4. Project Scope

Boulifa Mohamed Salah Eddine

AutiGuide will be developed as a web-based or desktop chatbot application within the timeframe of this course. The scope is carefully defined to ensure a high quality and fully functional deliverable within available group resources.

In Scope:

- A retrieval-based chatbot that accepts and processes English language input only, matching user queries to predefined responses using NLP techniques including tokenization, lemmatization, keyword extraction, and pattern matching, consistent with the approach validated by Jusoh et al. (2024) in the HelpBot system
    
- A structured English language knowledge base containing at least 100 question-and-answer pairs organized across five core domains:
    

- Behavioral Management — evidence-based strategies for handling meltdowns, sensory overloads, repetitive behaviors, and sleep difficulties
    
- Therapy & Intervention — accessible explanations of Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), speech therapy, occupational therapy, and early intervention programs
    
- Dietary Guidance — common dietary considerations, food sensitivities, and nutritional recommendations relevant to autistic children
    
- School & Social Support — practical strategies for collaborating with teachers, developing social skills, and navigating inclusive education in Malaysia
    
- Malaysian Resources — curated directory of local support groups, clinics, NGOs, and government programs including Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat services
    

- A clean and user-friendly English language chat interface accessible via web browser or desktop application
    
- A fallback response mechanism for queries outside the knowledge base scope that responsibly directs users to consult a qualified professional
    
- Basic conversation logging to identify common query patterns for future system improvement
    

Out of Scope:

- AutiGuide will not supply any form of clinical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice under any circumstances, responses are for informational and educational purposes, and should be treated as advice only
    
- The system will not provide real-time appointment booking or any forms of integration with healthcare providers or government databases
    
- Generative AI will not be used in responses, all responses will be acquired exclusively from verified and curated databases
    
- Multilingual support is not included in this version, the system will only accept and respond only in English
    
- The system will not collect or store any personal data about the users (child, parents), in order to comply with privacy practices.
    

### Target Users:

Ahmed Rana Saim

AutiGuide is developed in order to assist the various kinds of users that engage in contact with autistic children diagnosed with ASD. In this regard, AutiGuide places its emphasis on helping provide easily accessible and immediate guidance for those who work with autistic children.

#### 1. Primary Users – Parents and Caregivers

AutiGuide’s key users are the parents, guardians, or caregivers of children diagnosed with autism spectrum disorders (ASD). The intended users are the key targets since they bear full responsibility for the children’s care and behavior management. The challenge that many caregivers encounter is in dealing with autism symptoms such as meltdown, routine management, and accessing reliable information. AutiGuide assists the caregivers to get timely and research-based information wherever and whenever necessary.

#### 2. Secondary Users – Teachers and School Counselors

The secondary users include teachers, counselors in schools, and other educators who are constantly dealing with autistic children within an academic setting. They may need some direction on how to communicate, manage classroom behavior, engage socially, and implement inclusive education for the benefit of the autistic children. AutiGuide can help such users through its easy access to relevant data.

#### 3. Tertiary Users – Social Workers and Support Organizations

Tertiary users of the system are those who work as social workers, autism support groups, NGOs, and welfare agencies involved in providing services related to disabilities. They could use AutiGuide as an informational source while helping the families dealing with autism issues, creating awareness, and helping them learn about the support services available in Malaysia.

#### User Needs and Benefits

Users of AutiGuide need a technology system that will be user-friendly and readily available at all times. The chatbot offers instant feedback to its users while at the same time eliminating the need for accessing dubious material from the Internet. This will result in providing users with accurate information, empathy, and science-based support, hence increasing their confidence and awareness.

### Technology Stack:

Sofiane Mohammed Gherrat 

AutiGuide will be developed using Python as the primary programming language. The Natural Language Toolkit (NLTK) library will handle local text preprocessing including tokenization, lemmatization, and part-of-speech tagging. The knowledge base will be structured in JSON format for efficient pattern matching and response retrieval. The user interface will be built using Flask for web deployment or Tkinter, Pyside,  for desktop deployment.

The system will operate entirely without external APIs, cloud-based AI services, or third-party chatbot platforms. All processing runs locally on the host machine, mirroring the template-based local NLP architecture validated by Jusoh et al. (2024) in HelpBot. This ensures full transparency, reproducibility, and academic integrity throughout development.

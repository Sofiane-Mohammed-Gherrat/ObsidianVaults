
**1-2. The Property** – Tenant has 3 days to inspect the property from commencement; no repairs required from landlord after that. Property taken on "as-is-where-is" basis.

**3. Deposits** – Security and Utilities Deposits given to landlord.

- Security Deposit covers damage beyond fair wear and tear; tenant must replenish within 7 days of deduction notice; excess damage forfeits deposit and landlord may sue for balance.
- Utilities Deposit covers unpaid utility bills outstanding over 2 months; same replenishment/forfeiture rules apply.
- Deposits refunded within 14 days of property return if no deductions needed.
- Deposits cannot be used as rent payment.

**4. Tenant's Responsibilities** – Pay rent by the 12th of each month and notify landlord via text; ==pay all utilities and service charges (except maintenance/sinking fund/insurance)==; allow landlord entry with 72 hours' notice; use property lawfully and residentially only (no business); no signage/painting on walls; no subletting/assigning; return property in good condition with keys/access cards; avoid voiding insurance; maintain property in tenantable condition; need landlord permission for modifications; no dangerous/hazardous materials; comply with building Deed of Mutual Covenants; service air-con/curtains/cleaning at least annually; pay for lost access cards; no incense/smoke-emitting items. Tenant liable for landlord's legal costs if sued for recovery of unpaid amounts.

**5. Landlord's Responsibilities** – Maintain structure/exterior and facilities; pay assessment and quit rent; allow peaceful enjoyment if tenant complies; return deposits within 14 days after property returned (minus deductions).

**6. Tenant Default** – Landlord may terminate and forfeit deposits if: rent unpaid 14+ days, tenant breaches duties after warning, tenant has bankruptcy-related orders/arrangements with creditors, or goods seized on the property.

**7. Early Termination** – Tenant must give 2 months' written notice; landlord has absolute discretion to accept, and may forfeit Security Deposit as penalty.

- 7.3: If rent unpaid within 30 days of commencement, landlord may terminate and security deposit becomes non-refundable.
- 7.4: If landlord cannot continue tenancy (personal issues/ownership transfer), deposit refunded plus 2 months' extra rent within 14 days of move-out.
- 7.5-7.6: Landlord may sell property during tenancy; tenant gets 2 months from SPA signing to vacate.

**8. Destruction by Fire** – Rent suspended if property substantially damaged by fire (not tenant's fault) until habitable again. Landlord not obligated to rebuild; if not rebuilding, must give 14 days' notice to terminate.

**9. Renewal** – Tenant gives 2 months' notice to renew; landlord has absolute discretion; if approved, renewed for 1 year at revised rent under same terms (excluding fire clauses 8(a)/8.1). If not renewing, tenant must allow landlord to advertise/show unit to prospective tenants.

**10. Joint Inspection** – Tenant arranges inspection 14 days before tenancy expiry; landlord provides itemized rectification list; tenant completes works within 30 days; landlord can accept/reject works (with supporting documentation); if tenant doesn't act within 7 days, landlord may hire own contractor and deduct costs from deposits, with tenant having dispute rights and 30 days to pay undisputed amounts.

**11-20. Other Terms**

- Agreement supersedes prior agreements/promises.
- Landlord's indulgence isn't a rights waiver.
- Governed by Malaysian law.
- Invalid provisions don't void the rest of the agreement.
- Notices via email/mobile phone.
- Legal process may be served via registered post, email, or WhatsApp PDF.
- Tenant pays all stamp duties/charges related to the agreement.
- Attempted property damage reported/prosecuted.
- If rent unpaid for a month past due date, landlord may padlock unit or terminate via WhatsApp notice.
- Interpretation: terms include successors/representatives; gender/number terms are interchangeable.

**Maintenance Checklist** – Splits repair responsibilities: landlord covers air-con spare parts (first service before handover) and water heater/flush system repairs (unless tenant negligence); tenant covers annual air-con servicing, lighting, drainage clogging, remote batteries, cabinet/window hardware, water taps, electrical items/cooker hood, curtains/blinds/carpets, and access card/key replacement (RM120 if lost/broken). At move-out, tenant must service air-con, repair items (fair wear excepted), settle utility bills, patch/repaint walls, and only use induction cooking (no gas cylinders).

**Inventory List** – Includes water heater with shower head, 1.0HP air-con with remote, single-door fridge, TV, electric cooker with hood, electric kettle, iron/ironing board, kitchen cabinet, bathroom mirror with LED, curtains, wardrobe, queen bed frame/mattress, study table/chair, shoe rack, and keys (main door, access card, bedroom door).

### ⚠️ Clause 10.4.1(3): 

**The agreement says:**
"...failing which interest at the rate of 5% per annum shall accrue on the outstanding amount."

**Replaced by**:
"Failure by the Tenant to pay any reasonable, properly documented and undisputed amount due within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice and supporting documentation shall constitute a breach of this Agreement, and the Landlord may pursue recovery of such amount through legal remedies available under applicable law."

**The agreement says:**
"Tenant agrees to fully indemnify landlord for all legal fees, filing fees, costs and whatever expenses incurred."

**Replaced by**:
"The Tenant shall be liable only for reasonable and documented legal costs, court fees and recovery expenses actually incurred by the Landlord in recovering unpaid rent, utility charges, repair costs, or other amounts properly due under this Agreement."

### ⚠️ Clause 10.4.1(1):

"Tenant will not challenge the cost of attending to the rectification works."

"The Tenant shall be entitled to review and reasonably dispute repair costs supported by invoices, receipts and quotations."

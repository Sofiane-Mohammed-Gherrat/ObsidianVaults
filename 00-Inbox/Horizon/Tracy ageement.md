**3.1.3** If at the point when the tenant returns the rented property the landlord discovers damage to it, landlord may set off an amount equivalent to the damage suffered from the security deposit.

**3.2** The Utility Deposit is to ensure that:

**3.2.2** In relation to 3.2.1, within 7 days of being notified of the deducted amount, the tenant must replenish the utilities deposit to the full deposit amount (refer Section 7); and  
**3.2.3** If at the point when the tenant returns the rented property the landlord discovers an outstanding utility bill or several, he may settle them and then set off an amount equivalent from the utilities deposit.  
**3.2.4** If an outstanding bill or several taken together are in excess of the utilities deposit, which is forfeited, the landlord has the right to sue the tenant for the balance.

**10.3.1** If the landlord rejects any part of the rectification works or its entirety, he may request the tenant to remedy those rejected parts or appoint his own contractor to remedy those rejected parts. The landlord may decide on either course in his absolute discretion.

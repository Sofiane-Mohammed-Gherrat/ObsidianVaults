
# Clauses Often Mistaken for Riba but Usually Are Not

### Security deposit

Many Muslims worry about deposits.

Generally:

- Refundable security deposits
- Utility deposits

are not considered riba by themselves.

The issue arises only if:

- interest is earned on them and claimed by a party, or
- they are used unfairly.

---

### Legal costs

Legal costs are usually compensation for actual expenses.

They are not automatically riba.

The concern is reasonableness, not interest.

---

### Damage claims

Claiming actual repair costs is generally compensation, not riba.

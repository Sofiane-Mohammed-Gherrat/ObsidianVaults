## What the majority of classical and contemporary scholars agree on

### 1. Additional money charged because a debt is paid late = Riba ❌

If a tenant owes:

- RM1,100 rent

and the contract says:

- pay RM1,100 if on time
- pay RM1,155 if late

or

- 5% interest accrues on unpaid amounts

then the additional amount is generally regarded as riba by many scholars. ([Islam-QA](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/590?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Late payment charges - Islam Question & Answer"))

This is why I recommended removing the 5% interest clause.

---

### 2. Recovery of actual legal expenses is different from interest ⚠️

Many scholars distinguish between:

**A. Interest / late-payment penalties**  
and

**B. Actual expenses incurred to recover a debt**

For example:

- Lawyer invoice: RM800
- Court filing fee: RM150
- Process server fee: RM50

Actual recovery cost: RM1,000

Some contemporary scholars allow recovery of these actual costs because they are reimbursement of real expenses, not payment for the passage of time. ([Islam-QA](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/506170?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Ruling on Paying a Penalty for Delaying Payment of a Professional Association Subscription - Islam Question & Answer"))

# Applying this to your tenancy agreement

The agreement has two separate issues:

## Clause 10.4.1(3)

> 5% per annum interest

This is the strongest Sharia concern.

Many scholars would classify this as riba. ([Islam-QA](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/590?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Late payment charges - Islam Question & Answer"))

I would recommend removing it.

---

## Clause 4.1

> Tenant indemnifies landlord for legal fees and expenses

This is different.

The clause does **not** say:

> pay additional money because time passed

Instead it says:

> reimburse legal expenses

That is why I did not automatically classify it as riba.

However, from a Sharia perspective, the wording is still problematic because it is extremely broad:

> all legal fees, filing fees, costs and whatever expenses incurred

Even if not riba, it may involve uncertainty or potential unfairness because there is no limitation on what can be claimed.

A better Sharia-friendly version would be:

> The Tenant shall be liable only for reasonable and documented legal costs, court fees and recovery expenses actually incurred by the Landlord in enforcing this Agreement.

If you want the safest possible position:

Ask the landlord to amend **both**:

That way you avoid:

- riba,

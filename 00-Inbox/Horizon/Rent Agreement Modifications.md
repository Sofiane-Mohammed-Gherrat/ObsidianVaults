
If I were signing this agreement as a tenant, I would separate my requests into three categories:

1. **Must verify before paying anything**
    
2. **Must negotiate or amend**
    
3. **Nice-to-have improvements**
    

The first two categories are the important ones.

# MUST VERIFY BEFORE PAYING

## 1. Proof EZHOME is authorized by the owner

### Ask for:

- Letter of authorization from the owner
    
- Property management agreement
    
- Owner's name
    
- Evidence of ownership (title, assessment bill, quit rent receipt, utility bill)
    

### Reason:

The agreement is signed by a management company rather than the owner.

If the management company does not actually have authority to rent the property, you could end up paying deposits and rent to someone who has no legal right to lease the unit.

### Suggested wording:

> Please provide documentary proof that EZHOME Consulting & Management Sdn Bhd is authorized by the property owner to enter into this tenancy agreement and collect rental and deposits on the owner's behalf.

---

## 2. Ask who will hold the deposit

### Ask:

- Will the deposit be held by EZHOME or the owner?
    
- Who refunds the deposit at the end?
    

### Reason:

You want a clearly identifiable party responsible for returning RM2,125.

---

## 3. Ask for inventory and condition report

### Ask:

- Inventory list
    
- Existing defects list
    
- Photos of current condition
    

### Reason:

Without a move-in condition report, almost any damage can later be blamed on you.

This is one of the most common causes of deposit disputes.

# MUST NEGOTIATE / MODIFY

## 4. Remove utility disconnection rights

### Current clause:

Management reserves the right to disconnect water and electricity.

### Ask:

Remove entirely.

### Reason:

A landlord should pursue lawful recovery of unpaid rent instead of cutting essential services.

This clause gives them a powerful tool to pressure tenants during disputes.

### Suggested replacement:

> The Management shall pursue any recovery of unpaid rent through lawful means and shall not disconnect utilities except where required by law or the utility provider.

---

## 5. Remove lockout / sealing rights

### Current clause:

Management may:

- seal premises
    
- attach locks
    
- re-enter premises
    

### Ask:

Delete these provisions.

### Reason:

A landlord should not be able to physically exclude a tenant without proper legal process.

This is one of the most concerning clauses in the agreement.

### Suggested replacement:

> Any eviction or recovery of possession shall be conducted in accordance with applicable Malaysian law.

---

## 6. Reduce 30% late payment penalty

### Current clause:

30% penalty after the 13th day.

### Ask:

Replace with:

- Fixed administrative fee (RM50–RM100)  
    or
    
- 5% maximum late payment charge
    

### Reason:

30% is excessive.

Example:

RM850 rent × 30% = RM255

That is a very large penalty for being a few days late.

---

## 7. Clarify early termination

### Current contradiction:

Schedule:

> Move Out Early One Month Notice

Clause 5.2:

> Three months rental damages

### Ask:

State clearly which one applies.

### Reason:

These clauses conflict.

You need certainty before signing.

### Suggested wording:

> Tenant may terminate the tenancy by giving one month's written notice and forfeiting one month's rental, with no further liability.

or whatever you agree to.

---

## 8. Limit deposit deductions

### Current issue:

Management has broad discretion to deduct from deposit.

### Ask:

Add:

> Deposit deductions shall be limited to:
> 
> - unpaid rent
>     
> - unpaid utilities
>     
> - actual repair costs beyond fair wear and tear
>     
> 
> Supporting invoices or receipts shall be provided.

### Reason:

Prevents arbitrary deductions.

---

## 9. Define cleaning deductions

### Current issue:

Unit can be deemed "dirty" and cleaning fees deducted.

### Ask:

Specify:

> Maximum cleaning deduction RM___ unless extraordinary cleaning is required.

### Reason:

Currently they can decide any amount.

---

## 10. Define deposit refund deadline

### Current clause:

Deposit refunded after utilities are issued.

### Ask:

Add:

> Deposit shall be refunded within 14 working days after final utility charges are known and in any event no later than 30 days after vacant possession.

### Reason:

Protects against indefinite delays.

---

# REPAIR & MAINTENANCE ISSUES

## 11. Limit tenant responsibility for repairs

### Current clause:

Tenant responsible for many repair categories.

### Ask:

Add:

> Tenant shall only be responsible for damage caused by misuse, negligence, or intentional acts.

### Reason:

Normal failure due to age should not automatically become your responsibility.

Example:

- old toilet flush fails
    
- old switch burns out
    
- aging pipe leaks
    

These should not automatically become tenant costs.

---

## 12. Air-conditioning servicing

### Current clause:

All air-conditioning servicing at end of tenancy.

### Ask:

Clarify:

- number of units
    
- standard servicing only
    
- receipt requirement
    

### Reason:

Avoid disputes over what constitutes "servicing."

---

## 13. Existing defects

### Ask:

Add:

> Tenant shall not be responsible for defects existing prior to commencement of tenancy.

### Reason:

Very important protection.

---

# OCCUPANCY ISSUES

## 14. Clarify maximum occupancy

### Current clause:

Maximum 10 persons.

### Ask:

Specify:

> Maximum 10 overnight occupants.

### Reason:

The current wording is vague.

Technically it could be interpreted to include visitors.

---

# LIABILITY ISSUES

## 15. Narrow the indemnity clause

### Current clause:

Tenant indemnifies management for a very broad range of claims.

### Ask:

Add:

> Tenant shall only be liable for claims arising from the Tenant's negligence, breach of agreement, or unlawful acts.

### Reason:

Current wording is overly broad and may shift excessive liability onto you.

Correct dates before signing.

---

## 17. Consistent terminology

The agreement alternates between:

- Management
    
- Landlord
    
- Management Company
    

### Ask:

Define each term clearly.

### Reason:

In a dispute, ambiguity benefits whoever drafted the agreement.

---

## 18. Ensure stamping

### Ask:

Who will arrange stamping?  
When will stamped copy be provided?

### Reason:

A stamped tenancy agreement has significantly stronger evidential value.

# What I would personally insist on

If I were the tenant, I would not sign until these five items were addressed:

### Priority 1

- Proof EZHOME is authorized by owner
    

### Priority 2

- Remove utility disconnection clause
    

### Priority 3

- Remove lockout/sealing clause
    

### Priority 4

- Clarify one-month notice vs three-month penalty contradiction
    

### Priority 5

- Obtain signed inventory and condition report with photos

Those five items present the largest practical risks to you. Most deposit disputes, eviction disputes, and rental scams stem from one of those areas rather than the smaller clauses.

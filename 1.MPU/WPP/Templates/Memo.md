
# Traditional

**MEMORANDUM**

*To:* All Staff
*From:* Human Resources Department
*Date:* 27 March 2026
*Subject:* Office Attendance Policy Reminder

Dear Staff,

This memo serves as a reminder regarding the company’s attendance policy. All employees are expected to report to work on time and follow the official working hours.

Repeated lateness may result in disciplinary action. If you are unable to attend work, please inform your supervisor as early as possible.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Sincerely,
Human Resources Department

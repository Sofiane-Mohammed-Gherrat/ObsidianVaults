
> **Letter Full Block**

[Your Name]  
[Your Address]  
[Date]  

[Receiver’s Name]  
[Company Name]  
[Company Address]  

Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],  

Subject: [Clear and specific title]

[Paragraph 1 – Introduction]  
State your purpose clearly (why you are writing).

[Paragraph 2 – Details]  
Explain the situation, provide details, reasons, or background.

[Paragraph 3 – Conclusion]  
State what action you want + polite closing.

Yours sincerely,  
[Your Name]

---

> **<u>Routine Letter:</u>**
   P → D → A
   Purpose
   Details
   Action
   ROUTINE LETTER TEMPLATE
   (Used for: requests, enquiries, confirmations, placing orders, etc.)

[Your Address]
[Date]

[Receiver’s Name / Company]
[Company Address]

Dear [Sir/Madam or Name],

I am writing to [state your purpose clearly].

[Provide details: explain your request / information / situation clearly].

I would appreciate it if you could [state action needed]. Thank you for your attention and I look forward to your response.

Yours faithfully/sincerely,
[Your Name]


> **<u>Non-Routine Letter:</u>**
   Buffer → Problem → Solution
   Start politely (no harsh tone
   Explain problem
   Suggest solution / action
   NON-ROUTINE LETTER TEMPLATE
   (Used for: complaints, adjustments, bad news, sensitive issues)


[Your Address]
[Date]

[Receiver’s Name / Company]
[Company Address]

Dear [Sir/Madam or Name],

I am writing regarding [state issue politely].

[Explain the problem clearly with necessary details, but remain polite and professional].

I hope you can [state solution / action expected]. I trust that this matter will be resolved promptly. Thank you for your understanding.

Yours faithfully/sincerely,
[Your Name]

# Comparaison Table 

| **Feature**           | **Routine Letter (Direct)**                                    | **Non-Routine Letter (Indirect)**                               |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Goal**      | Efficiency and clarity.                                        | Diplomacy and rapport-building.                                 |
| **Structure**         | **P**urpose $\rightarrow$ **D**etails $\rightarrow$ **A**ction | **B**uffer $\rightarrow$ **P**roblem $\rightarrow$ **S**olution |
| **The "Hook"**        | States the main point immediately.                             | Starts with a neutral or positive buffer.                       |
| **Tone**              | Informative, brisk, and professional.                          | Empathetic, tactful, and persuasive.                            |
| **Typical Use Cases** | Orders, inquiries, and meeting invites.                        | Complaints, refusal of credit, or bad news.                     |
| **Emotional Impact**  | Low (Neutral/Positive).                                        | High (Potentially Negative).                                    |
